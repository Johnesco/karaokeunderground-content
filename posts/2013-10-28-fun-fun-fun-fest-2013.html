Want to go to Fun Fun Fun Fest 2013? Want to help provide Texas prisoners with quality reading material? Do both this Saturday!

Karaoke Underground will be part of the festival fun at Mohawk on 11/9, Saturday NITE after Tenacious D, Craig Robins, Jenny Slate and Matt Bearden, and we have a pair of 3-day passes to give away at our First Saturday show at Nomad! You can enter in two ways:

1. Donate $5 to Inside Books, who will have a table at Nomad. They’re a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, the only books-to-prisoners program in Texas, where over 140,000 people are incarcerated. Inside Books Project works to promote reading, literacy, and education among incarcerated individuals and to educate the general public on issues of incarceration.
