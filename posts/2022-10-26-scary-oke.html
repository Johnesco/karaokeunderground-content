Thursday 10/27 is the first time KU is hosting an actual Halloween-themed show, don’t miss it! It’s part of Phantom Fest and it’ll be at Central Machine Works, with a costume contest and a focus on the spookiest, scariest and creepiest songs in the KU list. Plus some cute ones. Check out the KU Scary-oke playlist on Spotify.
