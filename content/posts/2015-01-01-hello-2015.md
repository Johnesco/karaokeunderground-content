2014 was an unbelievable year for Karaoke Underground, thanks to every single one of you who sang with us! Hannah and I started this thing on December 31st, 2003, in our living room simply because we wanted to share some of our favorite songs with friends and meet some more fun people in our new hometown – people who weren’t afraid to be seen having fun. It’s come such a long way since then, and we’ve been lucky enough to make a lot of those friends we hoped we’d find, and even to meet and talk to some of the people who inspire us with their music.

The biggest kick for me is still watching someone’s eyes light up when they first open the list and realize that their favorite songs – the ones that mean something personal, that maybe they felt like an outcast for liking, or were resigned to keeping private or sharing with just a few close friends – are in there, and they have a chance to share that enthusiasm with a roomful of people who love the music too. It’s a simple pleasure, and we’ve all got so many stories and reasons why we like our favorites, but I’m incredibly grateful to you all for sharing it.
