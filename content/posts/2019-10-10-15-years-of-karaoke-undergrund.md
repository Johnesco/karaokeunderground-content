Come celebrate 15 years of Austin’s best karaoke shows with Karaoke Underground and Karaoke Apocalypse! We’ll be alternating hour-long sets on Saturday, 10/12, starting with Karaoke Underground at 9 o’clock, come sing it LOUD!
