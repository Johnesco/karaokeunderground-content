Thursday 10/27 is the first time KU is hosting an actual Halloween-themed show, don’t miss it! It’s part of [Phantom Fest](https://www.austinchronicle.com/arts/2022-10-21/a-season-for-scaring-at-phantom-fest/) and it’ll be at [Central Machine Works](https://www.cmwbrewery.com/), with a costume contest and a focus on the spookiest, scariest and creepiest songs in the KU list. Plus some cute ones. Check out the [KU Scary-oke playlist on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/6iA5qgZnFXJnE7MlRyEiPM?si=f7b1f41a6e9642b7).

The whole songlist will be available of course, get ready for a fun early show from 8-11 and sing it LOUD!
