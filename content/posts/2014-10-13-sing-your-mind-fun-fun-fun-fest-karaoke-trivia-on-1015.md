Very excited for this [Fun Fun Fun Fest-themed](http://funfunfunfest.com) show at the [North Door](http://ndvenue.com) on Wednesday night! [Pop Quiz](https://twitter.com/PopQuizATX) will host a FFF trivia contest from 7-9 and then we’ll have Karaoke Underground from 9-midnight, featuring 86 songs from artists appearing at the fest this year. We’ll have grand prizes of FFF passes for the trivia winners and as a raffle prize during karaoke, plus food and drink prizes as well. $5 cover and raffle ticket proceeds will benefit [SafePlace](http://safeplace.org) so come SING YOUR MIND for tons of fun and a great cause!

[Pop Quiz](https://twitter.com/PopQuizATX) @ 7p – Fun Fun Fun Fest Trivia\
Karaoke Underground @ 9p – punk/indie karaoke w/ FFF artists\
FFF passes for trivia and raffle winners, plus more prizes!\
Get raffle tickets by singing songs from FFF artists and/or making a donation to [SafePlace](http://safeplace.org).\
[FB event](https://www.facebook.com/events/699253373461041/)
