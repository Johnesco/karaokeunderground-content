A few hours after we close at Nomad this Saturday night, I’ll be hopping a plane to the West Coast for KU’s first show on the Pacific, at the first annual [PRF BBQ West](http://www.prfbbq.org) in Oakland, California! KU will be there on Sunday night, May 4th, closing out the three-day festival that features great bands like The Gary, Thoughts Detecting Machines, Nonagon and Victory & Associates. Check out the full lineup at [http://www.prfbbq.org](http://www.prfbbq.org), which includes links to Bandcamp streams for every artist playing.
